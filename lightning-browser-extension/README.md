# 👾 Features

#### With Alby Browser Extension, you can:

🧩 manage multiple Bitcoin Lightning wallets

🧩 use Bitcoin and Nostr apps on the web (your Bitcoin & Nostr companion!)

🧩 connect to your own node or Alby Account

🧩 recover your Bitcoin or Nostr private key with a Master Key, a 12-word seed phrase

All hosted on your device, no one sees your keys & actions
